About us

Our school

Ten years of teaching in Achampet

New Exceed High School is in Teachers Colony, Achampet, Nagarkurnool district. In February 2026 the school marked a decade of excellence. The aim has stayed the same: good teaching, daily encouragement, and a calm place to learn.

Students prepare for school examinations and for IIT and NEET foundation, with teachers and parents working as partners.

Our mission

To give quality education with discipline, care and good values, so children can do well in school and grow in character.

Our vision

Students who think clearly, work hard, speak with confidence and treat others with respect.

What we value

The habits we keep

Discipline

Punctuality and respect in the classroom, on the campus and at every event.

Curiosity

Questions are welcome. Spoken English and activities keep learning alive.

Partnership

Teachers and parents stay in touch about progress, tests and conduct.
